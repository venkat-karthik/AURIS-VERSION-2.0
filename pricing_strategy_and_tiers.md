## 1. Executive Summary

Auris provides **Autonomous AI Voice Employees** that handle inbound and outbound phone conversations with human-grade naturalness and sub-300ms response speed. 

Unlike traditional interactive voice response (IVR) systems or generic chatbots, Auris voice employees converse fluently in **English, Hindi, and regional Indian languages (Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi)**, understand nuances and colloquial phrasing, answer inquiries from your business knowledge base, qualify leads, and autonomously book appointments directly into your calendar and CRM.

## 3. What Every Auris Plan Includes

Regardless of the tier chosen, every Auris customer receives our full enterprise-grade core engine:

* **Fair 20-Second Billing Increment:** Calls are metered in precise 20-second smart slots rather than being rounded up to full minutes. Businesses save 25% to 35% on unutilized airtime compared to legacy telecom providers.
* **TRAI & DLT Compliant Telephony:** Official Indian (+91) virtual landline and mobile numbers provisioned through licensed Class-A telecom carriers.
* **Ultra-Low Latency Conversational Engine:** Sub-300ms turn-taking with intelligent voice activity detection (Silero VAD), allowing callers to interrupt naturally without robotic pauses.
* **Dual-Track Audio & Transcripts:** High-fidelity recordings separating customer and AI audio tracks for quality auditing and compliance.
* **Instant Post-Call Intelligence:** Every call automatically generates a structured summary, lead qualification score (1–100), key caller objections, and identified next steps.
* **Live Human Call Handoff:** Configure intelligent transfer rules that seamlessly patch warm calls through to your human sales or support managers when a high-intent customer requests immediate assistance.

## 8. Billing, Compliance & Service Terms

1. **Payment Terms:**
   - Retainers and minimum commitments are billed monthly in advance via Razorpay Auto-Debit (e-Mandate, Cards, Net Banking, or UPI).
   - In Enterprise Flex, pay-as-you-go overages are calculated at the end of each billing cycle and settled against the registered payment method.
   - All fees are quoted exclusive of applicable GST (18%). GST-compliant tax invoices are issued automatically upon transaction completion.
2. **Pulse & Call Metering Policy:**
   - Minutes are counted strictly on connected, answered calls. Unanswered, busy, or invalid numbers incur zero minute charges.
   - Billing increments are calculated in **20-second smart slots**, ensuring fair and transparent usage metering.
3. **Rollover & Validity:**
   - Included monthly plan minutes renew at the beginning of each 30-day billing cycle.
   - Pre-paid Top-Up Packs do not expire and remain available as long as an active subscription tier is maintained.
4. **Regulatory KYC & TRAI Compliance:**
   - Phone numbers are provisioned under Department of Telecommunications (DoT) regulations. Clients must provide standard company KYC documentation (GST Certificate, Company PAN, and Authorized Signatory ID).
   - Outbound calling campaigns strictly respect National Do Not Disturb (DND) registries and TRAI operating windows (9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST).
5. **Contract Flexibility:**
   - Monthly plans carry no long-term lock-in and can be canceled or adjusted with 15 days' notice prior to the renewal date.
